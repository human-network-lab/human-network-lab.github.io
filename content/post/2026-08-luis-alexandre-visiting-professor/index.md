**Luís Alexandre**, Full Professor at the Department of Informatics, Universidade da Beira Interior (UBI) in Covilhã, Portugal, will join us as a visiting professor between **September 1 and November 30**.

Luís works on neural networks, computer vision, and pattern recognition. During his stay, we will be working together on the networks of musical structure and brain-body interaction.
