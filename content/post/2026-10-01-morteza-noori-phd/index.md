We are happy to welcome **Morteza Noori**, who is officially joining the lab as a PhD student at the Interdisciplinary Doctoral School of Academia Copernicana, Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń.

Morteza comes to us with a background in biomedical engineering, where he worked on continuous stress estimation from multimodal physiological signals. In his PhD he will study how stress unfolds across the brain and the body, bringing together neuroimaging and physiological measures with machine learning and network methods. Welcome, Morteza!
