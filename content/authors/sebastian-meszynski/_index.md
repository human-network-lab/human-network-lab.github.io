## Background

I am an engineer and researcher working at the intersection of automation, biomedical engineering, computational modelling, and human–machine systems. My research combines mathematical and computational models with experimental approaches to study the dynamics of physiological systems, with a particular focus on the respiratory system. I am especially interested in describing complex biological systems as networks of interacting components operating across different spatial and temporal scales.

My work also draws on experience in control systems, robotics, sensor technologies, and the development of experimental and simulation platforms for biomedical research and education.

## Interests

- Computational modelling of physiological systems
- Multi-agent and multiscale modelling
- Respiratory biomechanics and patient-ventilator interaction
- Human-machine systems in biomedical engineering
- Biohybrid and artificial physiological systems
- Physiological digital twins
