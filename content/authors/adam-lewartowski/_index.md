## Background

I'm an interdisciplinary researcher, combining cognitive neuroscience, dynamical systems theory, and my own practice as a musician to discover how flow states can be primed. I work with virtual reality, audio and physiological recording, studying how people learn polyrhythms — two conflicting rhythms held at once by two hands.

## Interests

I am fascinated by the intersection of movement, embodiment, and mind. I am exploring the role of the fascial network and investigating how breathwork interventions and altered states of consciousness shape human experience.
